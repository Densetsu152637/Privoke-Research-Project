# Prospective reviewed clean-data augmentation

## Status and decision

Drafted 4 October 2026 against source `af91493b39ec9004ee22fc28c46f187ed4286c22`, before candidate-row access, preparation, fitting or new evaluation. All capacities, counts and seeds below are **proposed**, not observed. This document does not authorize automatic execution. Source eligibility, broad-target labels, exclusion preparation and the scoped implementation require review before fitting.

Question: does a fixed balanced augmentation with explicitly reviewed clean examples improve the **original contextual full pipeline**, when only its semantic contribution is gated by annotation-presence probability? Presence-only performance is secondary. This is not another three-profile search or fuzzer update study.

The [completed positive expansion](../../docs/PII-dataset-analysis.md) produced mixed specificity effects; [prior sparse updates](../../docs/presence-model-improvements.md) retained none. The [completed cascade](../../docs/contextual-cascade-results.md) improved the binary proxy but missed 90% specificity. These observations motivate a new data hypothesis, not a guaranteed improvement. Prior development inspection makes further tuning on that partition unsuitable; it is not used here. The final 498 examples remain unopened and unscored.

## Stage 4: one fresh test endpoint and retention

Score both frozen full pipelines once on the same fresh test rows, with no threshold, data, model or policy changes. Then evaluate both fixed arms on the existing 48-case contextual regression fixture, including only its four explicit visibility hints as inputs. Keep seven ambiguous cases qualitative and exclude unknown targets; use its reviewed allowed-actions/minimum-action rubric. Archive action downgrades and newly lost private WARN/BLOCK enforcement even when binary presence is unchanged.

Predeclare paired connected-component bootstrap: 2,000 resamples, seed `14102026`, both arms resampled together; percentile 95% interval for treatment minus control specificity. Undefined-class resamples are reported and excluded; require at least 1,900 valid replicates, otherwise no retention. No optional stopping or new resampling rule after results.

**Useful improvement** requires zero scoring/identity errors, treatment test recall >=90%, strictly positive lower bound of that paired specificity interval, and zero additional provisional private-action losses relative to control on eligible regression cases. Report recall difference and interval as well; the 90% floor does not imply recall noninferiority. **Joint target attainment on this endpoint** additionally requires treatment test specificity >=90%. Failure of any improvement criterion means no candidate retention and no tuning on test errors. Target attainment is separate from improvement, and neither certifies the original completion plan, real-world generalization, contextual correctness, deployment safety or professor approval. No default promotion is part of this experiment.

Presence-only probabilities/confusion, ordinary ungated and nonsemantic controls, ordinary/hard-negative strata, parent/component counts, paired detections/actions, excluded rows, internal/RPC latency and model costs are secondary/descriptive. The new source is disjoint from declared old sources by provenance/keys, but its test is within one synthetic generation family. Do not call it independent real-world or source-held-out generalization. No repeated scoring on the exposed original development set is needed.
