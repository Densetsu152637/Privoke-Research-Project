Phase 03 must have 12 terminal records, a frozen null winner, failed retention, exact restoration and no active failure or unknown update outcome. Reused validation/development do not establish untouched generalization. The protected final partition remains uninspected and unscored.

The grid is uniform default sampling or `contextual_role_quota_v1`, heads or final encoder block plus heads, and seeds 42, 1337 or 2026, at fixed learning rate 0.003. Each performs one 256-row training cycle with 16 whole-group held-out rows, no transformations and the existing 0.05 delta/gradient bound. Attempts do not accumulate training.

Original total weight, model preparation, severity/visibility/category targets, inference policy, and strict exact/class/severity/action/publication guards remain unchanged. Held-out metrics and safety guards remain row-based, with their original inputs/weights. Uniform refers to the existing default row sampler, not a claim that all original row weights equal one.

Severity counts remain separate coverage evidence. Default inventories retain their archived byte format. Select unique normalized texts without replacement, choosing source groups first and traversing variants in round-robin order. Fail on insufficient eligible rows/groups rather than filling quotas with duplicate or relabelled rows.
