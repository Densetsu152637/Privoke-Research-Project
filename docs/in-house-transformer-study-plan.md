## Questions and eight fixed arms

The new primary backbone question is whether **balanced full-encoder training improves full-pipeline specificity over matched balanced head-only training**, subject to the declared recall and provisional action-regression criteria. The existing augmentation question remains augmented sparse balanced C1 versus its frozen original sparse balanced C1 control. Neither comparison is a test of pretrained language modeling: all transformer encoder weights originate in repository-owned random initialization. No external pretrained weights, model APIs or language-model objective are used.

For each scratch arm use CPU Adam, learning_rate0.001, weight_decay0.0001, global gradient norm cap1.0, batch16, no dropout, scalar mean BCE, and no class reweighting or hyperparameter grid. Preserve exact row order initially; use a separate `numpy.random.default_rng(12102026)` order stream, generate one complete epoch permutation at a time, and share the recorded order/permutation commitments within each pair. Preserve partial evidence and restore prior catalogs. Online fuzzer fitting/cycles remain outside this experiment.

## Paired endpoint, hypotheses and retention

Score every frozen arm once on the same2,000-row test endpoint; all rows/class denominators and errors are reported. No model, threshold, labels, rule, policy, allocation or confidence method changes after access. Prespecified directional contrasts are **H-A: S1 minusS0 specificity** and **H-B: B-F minusB-H specificity**. H-B is the primary new backbone question; H-A preserves the existing augmentation question. No post-test best-profile or best-arm selection. Efficient/quality pairs and all cross-representation/profile differences are descriptive, with no confirmatory retention claim.

Use the base paired connected-component bootstrap:2,000 resamples with replacement of full test components, seed `14102026`, all rows of each sampled component and all eight arms resampled together. Sort canonical component IDs lexicographically; use `numpy.random.default_rng(14102026).integers(0,K,size=K)` once per replicate for K test components, with its NumPy/version/implementation commitment. Compute row-weighted confusion/rates within each replicate; undefined-class replicates are excluded/reported. Require at least1,900 valid replicates for each confirmatory specificity contrast, otherwise no confirmatory retention. Freeze percentile interpolation to NumPy `quantile(method="linear")`; report two-sided95% intervals and recall-difference intervals, with grouping/resampling qualifications.

For each of the two **directional improvement** claims require its95% interval lower bound>0. Each uses nominal one-sided alpha0.025; the two claims allocate total directional familywise alpha0.05 by Bonferroni. This retains H-A's existing95% criterion without treating two unadjusted one-sided5% tests as one family. Coverage/error control remains approximate under this finite component bootstrap, not guaranteed. Recall intervals are descriptive, not additional noninferiority tests. Other intervals are explicitly exploratory/descriptive and cannot justify candidate selection.

Research retention of S1 or B-F separately requires its predefined comparator, complete zero-error endpoint, treatment test recall>=90%, positive lower specificity-difference bound, and zero additional provisional private-action losses from the frozen regression check. Failure of either confirmatory contrast does not invalidate the other contrast's predefined result; report both, including null/regressing findings. E-F/Q-F and head-only references are not promoted through exploratory test results. An arm's **joint90/90 attainment** additionally requires its test specificity>=90%; improvement and attainment are different. The recall floor does not imply recall noninferiority to the comparator. No test-error-driven refit, extra seed, changed thresholds, default promotion or deployment is allowed.
