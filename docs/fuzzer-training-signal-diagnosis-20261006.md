# Training-signal diagnosis, 6 October 2026

The six completed fuzzer studies contain 126 attempts without a retained qualifying model. The latest decision-margin comparison improved pipeline specificity from 29.01% to 29.41% but left recall at 89.89%. This motivates a different training-signal hypothesis, not a claim that the hypothesis is proven or that a new model exists.

The frozen 2,300-row contextual curriculum contains 2,129 public annotation-negative rows, 43 bootstrap rows and 128 authored contextual rows. The authored rows divide 64 private/64 clean; bootstrap contains 33 typed-sensitive/10 clean. It contains no public annotation-positive training rows. `evaluation/prepare-public-negatives.py` maps annotation-negative examples to provisional S0/PU targets; absence of an annotation does not independently establish contextual truth. The quota sampler increases authored-positive exposure but does not add public annotation-positive supervision.

The expanded preparation manifest records native source revisions, positive-annotation checks, parent/family grouping and protected-overlap checks. Original TRAIN is an exploratory partition derived from PIIMB upstream-test data, rather than an independent benchmark training split. Meddies family grouping is coarse; its CC-BY-NC-4.0 attribution and non-commercial conditions remain attached. Added external sources are positive-only, so they cannot independently support specificity. Empty or missing source annotations are not automatically clean labels.

Independent TRAIN-only review confirmed the counts, prefix, hashes and overlap: original weak TRAIN overlaps the contextual curriculum by 782 groups and 117 normalized texts, whereas appended positives overlap neither. After the exact serving normalization, 13,732 expanded positives exceed the balanced model's 95 content-token capacity. Raw text token counts give a different answer at 38 boundary rows, so preparation must use serving normalization. These are prospective design inputs requiring frozen exclusion and token-visibility checks before fitting; source availability alone does not establish eligible supervision.

A prospective comparison can test identical authored contextual rows and balanced, fully visible weak presence rows with auxiliary coefficient zero versus one. Weak rows must have every contextual typed loss masked: annotation presence or absence must never fabricate sensitivity, visibility, categories or policy targets. Existing `ComputeSemanticGradients` accepts contextual examples only; `ComputePresenceGradients` trains a separate sparse model. A strict versioned interface and objective are therefore needed before this comparison can run.

The design must preserve the existing 16 contextual held-out cases and all publication/transport guards, add disjoint binary held-out groups with both labels and nonregressing recall/specificity, bind source provenance and group/ID/normalized-text exclusions, and keep winner-only endpoint evaluation. It must specify typed and auxiliary loss denominators separately. Even with typed losses masked, coupling weak annotation labels to the contextual detection union can change contextual decisions without establishing contextual truth; the fixture safeguards remain necessary. Model qualification remains at least 90% pipeline recall, measured specificity improvement and no new casewise fixture harms. No weak-label fit or score has been performed.

New preparation must consume frozen TRAIN files and permitted opaque exclusion indexes only. Do not rerun the legacy `prepare-public-negatives.py` entry point: its development/final loop reads protected final rows. Its existing source explains historical target construction, but does not authorize new final-data access.

Missing positive supervision is a plausible contributor, not an established cause of the plateau. Seeded representation quality, context truncation, provisional target mismatch, small effective updates and detector interactions remain competing explanations. The [results and quality index](model-quality-study-index-20261006.md) retains measured outcomes separately from this design hypothesis. Protected final rows and labels remain uninspected and unscored; the paper is unchanged.
